Mission briefing

How to use it

No account or setup needed. It takes about ten seconds.

STEP 01

Pick a photo

Choose a clear, well-lit photo of one face looking toward the camera. JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB.

STEP 02

Upload it

Drag the photo into the box below, or click the box to browse your files. Then press Analyze.

STEP 03

Read the result

You’ll see the most likely emotion and a pie chart of how the model split its confidence across four emotions. A high percentage is not a guarantee.

Photos are processed in memory and are not stored by this service. Only analyze photos of people who have agreed to it.

    Systems

    About the model

    A convolutional neural network (CNN) trained to sort faces into four emotions.

    Task

    Facial emotion classification into four classes

    Emotions

    Angry, Fearful, Happy, Neutral

    Input

    Color image resized to 300 × 200 pixels

    Architecture

    Three convolution and pooling blocks, one dense layer, softmax output

    Training data

    Chicago Face Database, a set of posed studio photos

    Built with

    TensorFlow and Keras, served with FastAPI

    Accuracy

    Read before you rely on it

    Ethical notes

    • This is a research prototype. Do not use it for medical, mental-health, or hiring decisions.
    • A facial expression is not the same as a feeling. The model labels how a face looks, not what a person is experiencing.
    • It was trained on posed photos, and neutral faces dominated the data (597 of 1,207 images), so it can be biased toward “Neutral.”
    • Get consent before analyzing anyone’s photo.
    • Photos are processed in memory and not stored by this service.
